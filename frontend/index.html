RRedlineResume Analyser

Resume + job description → score

Read your resume the way a screener does.

Upload a PDF or DOCX. Redline extracts your details, checks the structure, and compares the text with a job description using TF-IDF and cosine similarity. You get a score, the skills you are missing, and edits to make.

  1. 01 Upload your resume PDF or DOCX, up to 5 MB
  2. 02 Paste a job description optional, enables matching
  3. 03 Read the markup score, gaps, suggestions

Working

Parsing and scoring your resume…

Scores come from rule-based checks and TF-IDF cosine similarity. No trained model is used and nothing is stored on the server.
